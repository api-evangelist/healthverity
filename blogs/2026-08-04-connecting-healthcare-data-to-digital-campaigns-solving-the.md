Launching a pharmaceutical marketing campaign takes months of planning, creative development and media strategy, followed by rigorous research and refinement. When the campaign finally goes live, marketers need confidence they are reaching the right high-value audience across a fragmented landscape, that targeting and measurement are accurate and that results reflect real-world impact. That confidence depends on one critical factor: syncing real patient signals to digital identifiers, both when building audiences and when measuring outcomes.
