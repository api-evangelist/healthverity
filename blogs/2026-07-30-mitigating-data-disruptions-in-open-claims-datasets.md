Healthcare data now accounts for approximately 30% of global data volume and with this surge in data, life sciences organizations have access to more information than ever before. Some disruptions expand what you can do: new data owners entering the space, evolving collection methods and new data aggregators or suppliers. Others are the kind that can stall a project overnight, like a cyberattack across the industry or a data owner walking away from the commercial market.
