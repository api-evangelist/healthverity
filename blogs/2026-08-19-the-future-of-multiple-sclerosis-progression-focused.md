Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a chronic autoimmune disease affecting the central nervous system (CNS) and can lead to significant disability over time. It remains the most common inflammatory neurological condition worldwide and in the United States alone, approximately one million people are living with MS. Decades of drug development has yielded substantial advancements in suppressing inflammatory relapses, particularly for patients with relapsing–remitting MS (RRMS).
